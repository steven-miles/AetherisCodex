# Canon Decisions

This file records decisions that constrain future writing. Deliberate unknowns may also be recorded when preserving uncertainty is itself canonical.

## Setting

1. The world is bigger than any one player, hero, villain, or campaign.
2. Mystery should be preserved rather than automatically resolved.
3. Port Meridian is politically neutral.
4. Arcane Currents define skyship travel.
5. The Undertide influences politics but does not openly rule Port Meridian.

## Creative Direction

6. The horizon should remain more exciting than the completed map.
7. The player ship should be capable of becoming a recurring home and record of its crew's adventures.
8. Observable phenomena may have reliable practical rules without a known origin or complete explanation.

## Deliberate Unknowns

9. There is no canonical answer for what happens when a person falls into the Void.
10. Claims of returning from the Void may be introduced, but the Codex does not establish whether those claimants are truthful, mistaken, or fraudulent.
11. The ultimate origin and nature of Arcane Currents are unresolved.
