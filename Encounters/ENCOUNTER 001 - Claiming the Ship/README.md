# Claiming the Ship

## Purpose

This encounter teaches players:

- Ownership must be earned.
- Combat is only one solution.
- Intelligent opponents value their lives.
- The environment is part of the encounter.

## Success

The party leaves with a ship.

How they achieve that is entirely up to them.

## Failure 

Failure should create a new opportunity rather than end the Chronicle.

If they lose the encounter, the ship may leave, negotiations may continue, or another lead may appear.
