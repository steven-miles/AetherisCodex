# Chronicle Parties

The Tide Between Worlds is currently being played by two independent
adventuring parties within the same continuity of Aetheris.

The parties may experience similar events, but their decisions and
consequences are tracked independently unless an event is explicitly
established as affecting the shared world.

---

## Group 1

**Starting Level:** 5
**Party Size:** 6

### Campaign State

This is the original party for which the initial Session 1 encounter
balance was designed.

---

## Group 2

**Starting Level:** 3
**Party Size:** 3

### Campaign State

This party begins independently from Group 1 within the same Aetheris
continuity.

Encounters should be mechanically adjusted for the smaller and
lower-level party while preserving their narrative purpose.

---

# Shared Continuity

Both groups exist within the same Aetheris.

Events remain party-specific unless their consequences are significant
enough to alter the wider world.

Major world changes should be recorded separately so that their effects
can be reflected in the other group's Chronicle where appropriate.
