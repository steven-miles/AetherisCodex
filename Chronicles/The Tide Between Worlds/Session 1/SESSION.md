# Session 1 - Mechanics

> Narrative direction belongs in `SESSION_FLOW.md`.

---

## Beat 1 — Awakening

No required mechanics.

Avoid checks for basic observation.

---

## Beat 2 — First Steps

No required mechanics.

Registration is roleplay.

---

## Beat 3 — The Governor

No persuasion check is required for information he already intends to share.

Checks may be appropriate if players try to negotiate resources, pry into withheld information, or pressure policy changes.

### Governor's Token

The party receives a simple wooden token bearing the Governor's mark.

It may:

- establish that the party has his permission
- open conversations or restricted areas
- improve first reactions from officials
- remove unnecessary bureaucracy

It does not automatically win social encounters.

---

## Beat 4 — Loose Ends

Possible locations:

- tavern
- temporary barracks
- docks
- market
- travellers' quarter
- Fortune Teller's tent

Use roleplay first.

Only call for checks when failure would create an interesting consequence.

---

## Beat 5 — Finding a Way Forward

Possible checks:

- Persuasion
- Insight
- Investigation
- Deception
- Intimidation

Do not require all of them.

Let approach determine mechanics.

---

## Beat 6 — Claiming the Ship

Possible solutions:

- buy
- work for it
- negotiate
- persuade
- steal
- intimidate
- fight
- earn respect

### Optional Combat

If scavengers or a gang control the vessel, combat can occur.

The conflict is about ownership.

Opponents may surrender.

Non-lethal solutions are valid.

If the players pursue a hostile solution to acquiring a ship, see:

Encounters/Encounter 001 - Claiming the Ship

### Ship Condition

Old merchant vessel.

Cracked hull.

Repairable enough for Session 1.

---

## Beat 7 — First Departure

A small temporary crew helps the party leave Meridian.

Possible roles:

- steering
- navigation
- engine
- lines
- lookout
- deck work

Formal ship mechanics can be developed later.

### Milestone

Recommended milestone:

The party acquires its ship and departs Port Meridian.

### Session End

A distant island is visible ahead.

The party does not reach it during Session 1.
