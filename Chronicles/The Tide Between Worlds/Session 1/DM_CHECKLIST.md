# Session 1 - DM Checklist

> "Players remember how they felt, not what they rolled."

Use this checklist before every Session 1.

---

## Before You Begin

Aetheris is not a story you tell.

It is a world you present.

Your job is not to guide the players to the "correct" outcome.

Your job is to understand the world well enough that it responds naturally to whatever the players choose.

Don't rush moments.

Don't fear silence.

Trust your players to be curious.

Trust the world to respond honestly.

Whenever you're unsure what should happen next, ask yourself one question:

> **"What would naturally happen in Aetheris?"**

If you answer that honestly...

You'll almost always make the right decision.

# At the Table

## Before Beginning

☐ Introduce the world slowly.

☐ Allow silence.

☐ Let players investigate.

☐ Never rush a beat.

---

## During NPC Conversations

☐ Listen more than you speak.

☐ Let players ask questions.

☐ Allow pauses.

☐ Reward curiosity.

---

## During Combat

☐ Intelligent creatures protect themselves.

☐ Most people surrender before dying.

☐ The environment matters.

☐ Violence is one solution.

Never the only solution.

---

# Themes to Remember

☐ Wonder before danger.

☐ Curiosity before combat.

☐ Ordinary people make extraordinary places.

☐ Ships are homes, not equipment.

☐ Actions have lasting consequences.

☐ Every encounter should create one memorable moment.

---

# Session Goal

By the end of Session 1, the players should feel:

☐ Curious about the world.

☐ Attached to the Sea Bird.

☐ Interested in returning to Port Meridian.

☐ Excited to discover what lies beyond the horizon.

If all four are true...

The session was a success.

---

# Final Reminder

You are not telling the players a story.

You are introducing them to a world.

Trust them to decide what happens next.

---

# After the Session

Take ten minutes before changing any documents.

Ask yourself:

- What surprised me?
- What surprised the players?
- Which NPC did they enjoy most?
- Which moment made everyone smile?
- Did anything feel forced?
- What changed in the world because of the players?

Only then begin updating the repository.
