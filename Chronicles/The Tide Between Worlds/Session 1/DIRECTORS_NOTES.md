# Session 1 - Director Notes

> "This document exists to remind the DM how each beat should feel.
> It is not a script.
> It is a guide for performance."

---

# Beat 1 - Awakening

## Director's Intent

Introduce confusion, safety and curiosity.

The players should feel that something impossible has happened, but the people around them treat it as an ordinary morning.

Do not rush.

Silence is one of your most powerful tools.

---

## Performance Notes

Begin in darkness.

Do not describe what the players see.

Let them hear the world first.

Pause often.

Allow each sound to settle before moving to the next.

The magical engine should be heard long before anyone knows what it is.

Never explain it unless someone asks.

---

## Suggested Narration

> "The first thing you become aware of... is not light."

(Pause.)

> "It's sound."

(Pause.)

> "The slow groan of timber."

> "Ropes creaking somewhere overhead."

> "A bell rings in the distance."

(Pause.)

> "Then another."

> "Muffled voices."

> "Too quiet to understand."

(Pause.)

> "Beneath everything..."

(Pause.)

> "A steady rhythmic hum."

> "Constant."

> "Unfamiliar."

> "As though the ship itself is breathing."

(Pause.)

> "Your body feels heavy."

> "Your thoughts come slowly."

> "As your senses return, you realise you're lying on rough wooden boards."

(Pause.)

> "One by one..."

> "You open your eyes."

---

## The Hold

Allow the players to observe before speaking.

Do not mention the Voidmark.

If they notice another passenger's mark, let that discovery happen naturally.

If they inspect themselves, ask:

> "Where is your mark?"

Allow the players to define this themselves.

---

## Thomas

Thomas is the first Aetherian the players ever meet.

He is:

- Patient
- Efficient
- Kind

He never rushes frightened people.

He waits while people adjust to the light.

He never raises his voice.

He has done this many times already.

The impossible has become routine for him.

---

## Suggested Dialogue

## Suggested Performance

A uniformed dock escort appears at the top of the steps.

He looks around the room.

Counts everyone.

Satisfied, he nods.

> "Morning."

(Pause.)

> "Watch your footing coming up."

He turns and climbs the stairs.

He assumes people will follow.

## Director's Note

**Resident:** Thomas

The players do not know his name yet.

Only reveal it if they choose to ask him.

Thomas is patient, efficient and kind.

He has escorted many new arrivals over the past week, so while he understands the players' confusion, he does not treat the situation as extraordinary.

---

## Important Reminders

Do not explain Aetheris.

Do not explain the Void.

Do not explain why this is happening.

Allow the players to ask questions.

If Thomas doesn't know the answer, he simply says so.

# Beat 2 - First Steps

## Director's Intent

Reveal Aetheris through discovery.

The players should feel as though they discovered the world themselves.

Never force the reveal.

Guide their attention naturally.

---

## Performance Notes

As the players leave the hold, describe only the bright morning light.

Let them experience the relief of normality.

Blue sky.

Fresh air.

Only afterwards should the impossible reveal itself.

If the players do not naturally look outward, use the departing ship to draw their attention.

Never simply say:

> "You see the Void."

Earn the reveal.

---

## Suggested Performance

> "The morning sun catches your eyes as you step out onto the deck."

(Pause.)

> "Above you..."

> "Blue sky."

> "Clouds drifting lazily overhead."

> "For just a moment..."

> "Everything feels almost normal."

Allow them to react.

Once their attention shifts...

> "A deep harbour bell echoes across the docks."

> "A nearby skyship slowly begins to pull away from its berth."

Everyone nearby pauses.

Children wave.

Parents smile and wave too.

Crew salute the engine.

The answering bell rings.

The engine hum deepens.

The ship drifts effortlessly away.

Allow the players to follow it naturally.

Only then...

> "...and beyond the ship..."

> "...there is no sea."

(Pause.)

> "Only an endless black Void stretching beneath the world."

Pause.

Do not speak again until the players react.

---

## Thomas

## Suggested Performance

The dock escort pauses, allowing everyone a moment to take in the harbour.

Only once the group begins to move again does he quietly say:

> "Governor's expecting you."

He counts everyone once more before leading the way.

## Director's Note

**Resident:** Thomas

If the players ask his name, he simply replies:

> "Thomas."

No introduction.

No surname.

No ceremony.

Just Thomas.

---

## Ordinary Life

Never let the city stop for the players.

Cargo continues moving.

Merchants continue arguing.

Hammers continue striking.

People continue laughing.

Children continue playing.

The players are guests in a living city.

---

## Registration

Thomas leads the group towards a modest administrative building.

Several clerks process new arrivals.

The process is calm, organised and efficient.

Thomas does not ask questions.

The clerks do.

Suggested dialogue:

> "Name?"

(Pause.)

> "Place of origin?"

If a player names somewhere from another world:

The clerk simply writes:

> "Unknown."

No judgement.

No surprise.

Just another entry in the ledger.

---

## Edith

Edith is one of the clerks.

She is ordinary.

Ink-stained fingers.

Organised.

Busy.

She knows Thomas.

Their entire interaction should be no more than:

Thomas:

> "Busy?"

Edith:

> "Busier."

They both continue working.

Nothing more.

This is enough to show the city is alive beyond the players.

---

## Important Reminders

Do not rush the harbour.

Allow players to stop and look around.

The city should feel overwhelming without becoming chaotic.

The first memory of Port Meridian should be:

- A departing skyship.
- Ordinary people living ordinary lives.
- An impossible world that somehow feels completely real.
