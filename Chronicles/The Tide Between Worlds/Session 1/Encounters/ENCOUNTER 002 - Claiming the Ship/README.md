# Encounter 002 – Claiming the Ship

## Purpose

This encounter introduces Aetheris' combat philosophy.

The objective is not to defeat enemies.

The objective is to leave with a ship.

Combat is one possible solution.

Negotiation, intimidation, persuasion, stealth, bribery and clever thinking are equally valid.

---

## Encounter Pillars

- Ownership has value.
- Intelligent people protect what they believe is theirs.
- Most people do not fight to the death.
- The environment should matter as much as the enemies.

---

## Success

The players acquire a ship.

How they achieve that is entirely their decision.

---

## Failure

Failure should never end the Chronicle.

If the players fail to claim this ship, another opportunity should naturally emerge.

Failure changes the story.

It does not stop it.

---

## Design Notes

This is the first combat encounter many groups will experience in Aetheris.

It should teach players what combat feels like in this world.

Winning should feel satisfying.

Mercy should always remain an option.
