# Encounter 002 - Tactics

> "The Driftwrights are craftsmen first and combatants only when they have no other choice."

---

# Combat Philosophy

The Driftwrights do not seek violence.

They are ordinary working people protecting their livelihood, their colleagues and the Sea Bird.

If combat begins, they fight to end the conflict as quickly and safely as possible.

---

# Priorities

The Driftwrights always act according to the following priorities:

1. Protect life.
2. Protect the Sea Bird.
3. Protect Arthur.
4. Protect fellow Driftwrights.
5. End the conflict.

When making decisions during combat, always consider the highest priority currently under threat.

---

# Arthur

Arthur naturally takes command of the situation.

He attempts negotiation before violence whenever possible.

During combat he:

- Protects younger Driftwrights.
- Places himself between danger and his crew.
- Never attacks an unconscious creature.
- Never attacks a surrendered opponent.
- Orders surrender if continuing the fight would place unnecessary lives at risk.

If combat ends peacefully, Arthur immediately begins helping the injured regardless of which side they fought on.

---

# Veteran Driftwrights

Veteran Driftwrights fight with discipline rather than aggression.

During combat they:

- Form a defensive line.
- Protect the Sea Bird.
- Follow Arthur's instructions without hesitation.
- Use cover whenever possible.
- Avoid unnecessary risks.

If Arthur orders the fighting to stop, they immediately obey.

---

# Young Driftwrights

Young Driftwrights are inexperienced but determined.

During combat they:

- Stay close to one another.
- Protect fellow workers.
- Hesitate if badly injured.
- Surrender more readily than the veterans.
- Secretly admire the courage of adventurers.

---

# If Arthur Falls

If Arthur is reduced to 0 hit points:

- The Driftwrights immediately attempt to reach him.
- Combat becomes defensive rather than offensive.
- Their priority becomes stabilising Arthur and protecting him.
- If possible, they call for the city watch.
- They surrender rather than allow further unnecessary bloodshed.

---

# If A Young Driftwright Falls

Arthur immediately moves to protect them if able.

Nearby Driftwrights attempt to drag them to safety.

Protecting life becomes the immediate priority.

---

# If A Player Falls

The Driftwrights never attack unconscious characters.

If combat ends, they stabilise injured players before anything else.

No one is left behind to die.

---

# Fire

Fire is every Driftwright's greatest fear.

If a fire begins:

- Combat immediately becomes secondary.
- Everyone attempts to contain the flames.
- Even Arthur's enemies may find themselves working alongside him.

---

# Damage To The Sea Bird

Deliberately damaging the Sea Bird is the fastest way to escalate the encounter.

Every Driftwright immediately reacts.

Protecting the ship becomes their highest priority.

---

# Surrender

The Driftwrights always accept genuine surrender.

Once the fighting stops:

- Weapons are lowered.
- Injured people are treated.
- Conversation resumes.

The encounter does not need to end simply because initiative has ended.

---

# End Of Combat

Once the conflict has been resolved:

- The workshop slowly returns to normal.
- Workers continue their repairs.
- Conversations resume.
- The Driftwrights hold no unnecessary grudges.

The workshop is a place of work.

The sooner life returns to normal, the happier everyone is.
