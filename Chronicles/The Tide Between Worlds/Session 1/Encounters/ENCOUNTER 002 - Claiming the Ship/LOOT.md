# Encounter 002 - Loot

> "The Sea Bird is the reward.
>
> Everything else simply helps her sail."

---

# The Sea Bird

The Sea Bird comes fully equipped for a merchant voyage.

Everything aboard has been carefully maintained by the Driftwrights.

---

# Ship Equipment

- Anchor
- Mooring ropes
- Spare rope
- Spare sailcloth
- Navigation lanterns
- Ship's bell
- Cooking equipment
- Water barrels
- Food stores
- Basic repair tools
- Spare timber
- Spare fittings
- Empty cargo space

---

# Captain's Cabin

Inside the captain's cabin the party finds:

- A sturdy writing desk.
- A weathered chair.
- A map table.
- A compass.
- Quill and ink.
- A locked storage chest (currently empty).

---

# Captain's Log

An old leather-bound journal sits neatly upon the desk.

Most entries describe ordinary merchant life:

- Weather.
- Cargo.
- Repairs.
- Trade routes.
- Harbour notes.

Nothing within suggests great adventure.

The final readable entry simply reads:

> "She still has another voyage in her."

---

# Supplies

The Sea Bird carries enough food and fresh water for the opening stages of the Chronicle.

The stores are practical rather than luxurious.

---

# Coin

The ship contains no hidden treasure.

Ships are valuable because of what they can accomplish, not because of what they contain.

---

# Future Discoveries

As the Sea Bird is explored more thoroughly throughout the Chronicle, players may discover:

- Forgotten compartments.
- Personal belongings from former crews.
- Historical documents.
- Hidden repairs.
- Evidence of previous voyages.

These discoveries should be introduced naturally as the Chronicle progresses.
