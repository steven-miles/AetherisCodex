# Encounter 002 - Claiming the Ship
## Group 2 Variant

> The Driftwrights could overwhelm three people.
>
> That is exactly why they do not need to.

---

# Party

**Party Size:** 3

**Character Level:** 3

**Combat:** Optional

---

# Purpose

This configuration preserves the social and moral purpose of Encounter 002 while making physical escalation appropriate for a smaller, lower-level party.

The Driftwrights should still feel like:

- A real organisation.
- A populated workplace.
- People capable of defending themselves.
- People with every reason to protect the Sea Bird.

The party should not feel like the warehouse has been emptied simply because the group is smaller.

Most Driftwrights remain part of the scene without becoming active combatants.

---

# Important Principle

The Driftwrights have overwhelming numbers available.

They do not use them all at once.

They are not trying to kill three strangers.

Their first response to violence is:

**Contain it.**

Not:

**Crush them.**

---

# Creature Statistics

Use standard Driftwright Worker statistics from:

`../CREATURES.md`

Use Arthur's standard statistics.

Use the Group 2 Veteran below where required.

---

# Initial Response

If violence begins, start with:

- Arthur
- 2 Driftwright Workers

**3 active opponents**

Other Driftwrights remain nearby.

They may:

- Back away.
- Protect tools and ships.
- Move injured people.
- Call for help.
- Block civilians from entering.
- Watch nervously.

Do not roll initiative for them unless they become directly involved.

# Group 2 Driftwright Veteran

*Medium Humanoid*

The Group 2 Veteran is still clearly more experienced than an ordinary Worker, but is less mechanically dangerous than the standard Veteran.

---

**Armor Class:** 14

**Hit Points:** 30

**Speed:** 30 ft.

| 15 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) |

**Skills:** Athletics +4, Perception +4, Insight +4

**Passive Perception:** 14

## Actions

### Work Hammer

*Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.

**Hit:** 6 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

The Group 2 Veteran does **not** have Multiattack.

---

### Put Them Down

Instead of dealing damage with a successful Work Hammer attack, the Veteran may attempt to knock the target prone.

The target must succeed on a:

**DC 12 Strength or Dexterity saving throw**

or fall prone.

The target chooses the ability used.

---

## Reaction

### Not The Ship

When a creature within 5 feet attempts to deliberately damage a ship or important workshop equipment, the Veteran may move up to 5 feet and make one Work Hammer attack against that creature.

This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

---

# Escalation

Group 2's encounter should escalate slowly.

## Minor Aggression

Examples:

- A shove.
- A threat.
- A weapon drawn.
- One non-lethal attack.

Response:

- 1 Worker intervenes.
- Arthur attempts to stop the situation verbally.

Do not immediately begin a full combat.

---

## Combat Begins

If the party continues:

Active:

- Arthur
- 2 Workers

This should be the default Group 2 fight.

---

## Party Dominating

If the party is clearly overwhelming the initial defenders and continuing the attack:

Add:

**1 Group 2 Veteran**

This should feel like a meaningful escalation.

Describe an older or more experienced Driftwright stepping forward because the situation has become serious.

---

## Party Struggling

Do not add anyone.

Instead:

- Arthur calls for everyone to stop.
- Workers switch to grapples and restraint.
- Driftwrights create distance.
- The party is offered a chance to surrender or talk.

The Driftwrights have no reason to continue beating already-overmatched attackers.

---

# Maximum Recommended Active Opposition

For Group 2:

- Arthur
- 2 Workers
- 1 Group 2 Veteran

**4 active opponents maximum**

Do not exceed this unless the players deliberately create an extreme situation.

The wider Driftwright population remains present narratively.

---

# Arthur

Arthur uses his standard statistics.

However, for Group 2 his behaviour is especially important.

Arthur should prioritise:

- Grappling.
- Ordering people back.
- Moving allies.
- Talking during combat.
- Ending violence.

He should rarely spend turn after turn simply attacking the same character.

If he can restrain someone instead:

He probably will.

---

# Workers

Workers prefer:

- Grapples.
- Shoves.
- Blocking routes.
- Protecting the Sea Bird.
- Pulling people away.

They should not all focus attacks on the same player unless that person is doing something extremely dangerous.

---

# If A Player Falls

The Driftwrights stop attacking that character immediately.

If one of three player characters falls unconscious:

Reassess the encounter.

Unless the remaining party is still aggressively escalating:

- Arthur calls for a halt.
- Workers move the unconscious character somewhere safe.
- Someone stabilises them if necessary.
- Conversation becomes possible again.

---

# If Two Players Fall

The fight is effectively over.

The Driftwrights attempt to restrain or calm the remaining character.

They do not continue attacking simply to produce a total party defeat.

The party can lose the confrontation without losing the Chronicle.

---

# If The Party Surrenders

Combat ends immediately.

The Driftwrights:

- Disarm them if appropriate.
- Stabilise the wounded.
- Separate people.
- Allow Arthur to decide what happens next.

Arthur may be angry.

He is not interested in executions.

---

# If The Driftwrights Lose

The Driftwrights can surrender.

Arthur may order everyone to stop if:

- Continuing risks deaths.
- The party clearly controls the warehouse.
- The Sea Bird is already being taken.
- Further violence would achieve nothing.

The party then decides what kind of victors they are.

---

# Environmental Actions

Be generous with non-damage solutions.

Examples:

- Blocking a doorway.
- Cutting a line.
- Dropping tools or cargo to create distance.
- Disarming someone.
- Locking or barring access.
- Intimidating the room.
- Using magic to separate combatants.
- Protecting the Sea Bird from collateral damage.

Creative actions should be allowed to change the situation dramatically.

---

# Negotiation During Combat

Combat does not suspend roleplay.

Arthur and the Driftwrights will respond to:

- Apologies.
- Surrender.
- Offers.
- Explanations.
- Threats.
- Changes of heart.

A fight may become a negotiation at any moment.

Do not require one side to reach 0 hit points before talking resumes.

---

# Expected Experience

The party should understand:

The Driftwrights could make this much worse.

They are choosing restraint.

That communicates something important about Arthur and his people.

The central tension should remain:

**The Sea Bird matters.**

But so do the people standing in front of it.
