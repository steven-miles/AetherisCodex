# Encounter 001 - Creatures

> "The Driftwrights are not villains.
>
> They are ordinary people protecting something they believe is theirs."

---

# Encounter Balance

Designed for:

- 6 Level 5 Characters

Difficulty:

Medium

The Driftwrights are intentionally weaker than the party.

The challenge comes from the environment, player choices and protecting ownership rather than overwhelming combat strength.

Combat should feel exciting but fair.

---

# Driftwright Foreman

## Role

Leader

Negotiator

Protector

The Foreman would always prefer to settle disagreements without violence.

If combat begins, they focus on protecting their people rather than winning glory.

---

### Skills

### Actions

#### Salvage Hammer

+6 to hit

Reach 5ft

Hit:

1d8 + 4 Bludgeoning

---

#### Hook Throw (Recharge 5–6)

Throw a heavy salvage hook.

Range 20/60

Target makes a DC 14 Strength save.

Failure:

Pulled 10ft toward the Foreman.

---

### Bonus Action

#### Hold the Line

Choose one Driftwright within 30ft.

That creature may immediately move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

---

### Reaction

#### Protect the Crew

When an adjacent Driftwright is hit...

The Foreman imposes disadvantage on the attack by pulling them behind cover.

---

### Combat Behaviour

Never attacks unconscious creatures.

Never attacks fleeing enemies.

Will attempt negotiation if the battle clearly turns against the Driftwrights.

---

### Morale

If reduced below 20 HP...

or

More than half the Driftwrights are defeated...

The Foreman attempts to surrender.

# Encounter Behaviour

The Driftwrights never attempt to kill.

If a player falls unconscious...

They leave them where they fell.

They do not execute enemies.

---

The Driftwrights focus on:

- defending the Sea Bird
- protecting fellow workers
- stopping property damage

rather than defeating the party.

---

# Encounter End

Combat ends when one of the following occurs:

- The Foreman surrenders.
- The players surrender.
- Both sides negotiate.
- The Driftwrights retreat.
- The players claim the Sea Bird.

The objective is ownership.

Not annihilation.

---

# Design Notes

The Driftwrights are the first combat encounter in Aetheris.

They should teach players that:

- intelligent people value their lives
- surrender is normal
- negotiation remains possible after initiative begins
- environments matter
- every enemy has a reason for fighting

Players should leave respecting the Driftwrights, regardless of how the encounter ends.
