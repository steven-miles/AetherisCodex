# Encounter 001 - Consequences

> "The people of Aetheris remember."

---

# Peaceful Resolution

If the players earn the Sea Bird through trust and negotiation:

- Arthur gains respect for the party.
- The Driftwrights welcome them warmly in future.
- Joshua becomes increasingly excited by their adventures.
- The Governor is pleased with the outcome.
- Future repairs become easier to arrange.
- The Launch Bell rings proudly.

---

# Difficult Negotiation

If the players eventually reach an agreement after tense negotiations:

- Arthur remains cautious.
- Respect has begun but is not yet fully earned.
- The Driftwrights remain professional.
- Future trust must be built over time.

---

# Combat With Mercy

If combat occurs but the players show restraint:

- Arthur respects their honour.
- Relationships become strained but recoverable.
- The younger Driftwrights tell stories about the encounter.
- The Sea Bird may still become theirs.

---

# Excessive Violence

If the players use unnecessary force:

- Arthur struggles to trust them again.
- Repairs become more difficult to arrange.
- Driftwrights become noticeably cautious around the party.
- Joshua begins questioning whether they are truly the people he believed them to be.

---

# Murder

If the players deliberately kill surrendered or defenceless Driftwrights:

- Arthur (if alive) never fully trusts them again.
- The Driftwrights become hostile towards the party.
- Joshua is visibly disappointed.
- Thomas becomes noticeably more formal.
- The Governor's trust weakens.
- Rumours spread throughout Port Meridian.
- The Launch Bell is never offered to the party.

The world remembers what happened.
