# Encounter 001 - Raid on the Current
## Group 1 Variant

> Original encounter configuration.

---

# Party

**Party Size:** 6

**Character Level:** 5

**Target Difficulty:** Easy

---

# Purpose

This configuration is designed to give six Level 5 characters an opportunity to test their abilities without presenting a serious risk of party defeat.

The scavengers should appear numerous enough to represent a genuine boarding raid.

The encounter is not intended to test the party's survival.

If the party destroys the scavengers quickly:

Let them.

---

# Creature Statistics

Use the standard creature statistics from:

`../CREATURES.md`

No statistical changes are required.

---

# Encounter Composition

Use:

- 1 Scavenger Boss
- 6 Scavenger Raiders
- 2 Scavenger Scrappers

**9 potential hostile creatures**

Not every scavenger begins actively fighting the party.

---

# Starting Positions

## Passenger Vessel

Begin with:

- 3 Scavenger Raiders
- 1 Scavenger Scrapper

These scavengers have already successfully boarded.

---

## Gangplank / Crossing

Begin with:

- 2 Scavenger Raiders

They are crossing or preparing to cross.

They do not need to enter combat immediately if the battlefield changes before their turn.

---

## Scavenger Vessel

Begin with:

- 1 Scavenger Boss
- 1 Scavenger Raider
- 1 Scavenger Scrapper

The Boss initially attempts to control the raid from the scavenger vessel.

---

# Additional Scavengers

Other scavengers may be visible aboard the attacking vessel.

They are:

- Controlling the ship.
- Managing ropes.
- Securing the boarding route.
- Preparing an escape.
- Watching the raid.

They are not automatically combatants.

Do not add them simply to prolong the encounter.

---

# Reinforcements

If the party is overwhelmingly dominating the encounter and the players appear to be enjoying combat:

One or two additional visible scavengers may enter the fight.

This is optional.

Do not reinforce if:

- Combat is already satisfying.
- The party has used significant resources.
- Several characters are injured.
- The scavengers should logically be preparing to escape.

A quick victory is a valid outcome.

---

# Morale

Begin considering retreat when:

- 4 scavengers have been defeated.
- The Boss falls below half hit points.
- The Boss is defeated.
- The party displays overwhelming power.
- The scavenger vessel becomes threatened.
- The boarding routes are compromised.
- The party successfully intimidates the scavengers.

These are guidelines.

If continuing the raid is obviously foolish:

Call the retreat.

---

# Running The Fight

Group 1 has significant:

- Action economy.
- Hit points.
- Spell access.
- Area-of-effect potential.
- Battlefield control.

Do not compensate for this by making the scavengers tactically perfect.

Their poor judgement is part of the encounter.

A Level 5 ability that devastates several scavengers should feel devastating.

---

# Expected Experience

The party should begin combat thinking:

> "We're under attack."

The scavengers should eventually be thinking:

> "Why did we attack these people?"

That reversal is the heart of the encounter.
