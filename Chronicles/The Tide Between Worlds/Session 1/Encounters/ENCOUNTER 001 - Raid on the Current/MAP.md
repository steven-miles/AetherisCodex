# Encounter 001 - Raid on the Current
## Map Design

> Two ships.
>
> One narrow crossing.
>
> Nothing beneath them but the Void.

---

# Purpose

The battlefield should immediately communicate three things:

1. The party is aboard a functioning passenger vessel.
2. A smaller scavenger vessel has forcibly attached itself alongside.
3. There is an enormous open Void beneath both ships.

The map should provide opportunities rather than solutions.

Players should look at the battlefield and immediately begin thinking:

> "Can I use that?"

---

# Overall Layout

The encounter takes place across two vessels travelling alongside one another within the same Arcane Current.

The ships are roughly parallel but slightly offset.

The passenger vessel is:

- Larger.
- Better maintained.
- More stable.
- Designed to carry passengers safely.

The scavenger vessel is:

- Smaller.
- Rougher.
- Faster.
- Heavily repaired.
- Built from mismatched parts.
- Clearly less valuable.

The scavenger vessel has moved alongside the passenger ship and attached itself using grappling lines.

---

# Recommended Scale

Use:

**1 square = 5 feet**

The exact dimensions do not need to represent realistic shipbuilding.

Gameplay and readability are more important.

---

# Passenger Vessel

Recommended playable deck:

**Approximately 70 feet long × 30 feet wide**

Or:

**14 × 6 squares**

The vessel itself may be larger than the portion represented on the battlemap.

---

# Passenger Vessel Areas

## 1. Main Deck

The primary combat area.

Contains:

- Cargo crates.
- Barrels.
- Coiled rope.
- Ship equipment.
- Open deck space.
- Access below deck.

Keep enough open space for six player characters to move comfortably.

---

## 2. Passenger Hatch

The hatch leads below deck.

Most passengers have been moved here for safety.

This is where the players emerge at the beginning of the encounter.

The scavengers initially have no reason to enter the hold.

If they reach it, their goal is valuables rather than harming passengers.

---

## 3. Captain's Position

Captain Vex Sterling remains near the ship's controls.

This area contains the equipment required to keep the vessel travelling safely within the Current.

Vex rarely leaves this position during combat.

---

## 4. Cargo

Several crates and barrels are secured across the deck.

They may provide:

- Cover.
- Obstacles.
- Improvised weapons.
- Climbing opportunities.
- Something unexpected when the players inevitably decide crates are more interesting than swords.

Do not determine every possible interaction beforehand.

---

## 5. Railings

The edges of the vessel have sturdy railings.

Falling overboard should require something significant.

Normal movement near the railing does not require checks.

---

# Scavenger Vessel

Recommended playable deck:

**Approximately 50 feet long × 20 feet wide**

Or:

**10 × 4 squares**

It should feel noticeably smaller and more cramped than the passenger vessel.

---

# Scavenger Vessel Areas

## 1. Main Deck

The majority of scavengers begin here or cross from here.

The deck is cluttered with:

- Rope.
- Tools.
- Salvaged equipment.
- Empty containers.
- Recently stolen goods.
- Improvised repairs.

---

## 2. Helm / Engine Controls

At least one scavenger remains responsible for keeping the vessel alongside the passenger ship.

If the players interfere with these controls, improvise naturally.

The scavengers become extremely nervous if their vessel's ability to escape is threatened.

---

## 3. Boarding Area

This is where the majority of grappling lines originate.

The gangplank also connects from this section.

During retreat, this becomes the busiest area of the encounter.

---

# Position Between The Ships

Leave approximately:

**10–15 feet**

between the vessels.

The exact distance may change slightly as the ships move.

This gap is entirely open Void.

---

# Gangplank

A crude boarding plank bridges the gap between the vessels.

Recommended:

**5 feet wide**

This is the easiest route between ships.

Characters may cross normally unless circumstances make doing so difficult.

The gangplank can potentially be:

- Removed.
- Cut loose.
- Destroyed.
- Blocked.
- Defended.
- Used in ways nobody planned for.

Do not protect it because the encounter expects it to exist.

If the players remove it:

The encounter changes.

---

# Grappling Lines

Several heavy ropes stretch between the vessels.

Recommended:

**4 grappling lines**

Place them at different points along the ships.

This allows characters to potentially:

- Swing between vessels.
- Climb across.
- Cut the lines.
- Pull themselves closer.
- Use them to recover someone.
- Interfere with the scavenger vessel.

The scavengers use these ropes during boarding and retreat.

---

# Rigging

Both vessels have accessible rigging.

Players may climb it.

Do not require checks for ordinary climbing unless:

- They are rushing.
- They are under attack.
- The ship moves violently.
- They attempt something dangerous.

The rigging provides opportunities for vertical movement.

---

# The Void

Everything outside the ship decks is open Void.

Do not clutter the battlemap with environmental objects beneath the vessels.

The emptiness is part of the visual design.

The map should make the ships feel small.

---

# Starting Positions

## Player Characters

The party begins below deck.

When they choose to help, they enter through the passenger hatch.

Allow players to place themselves around the hatch as they emerge.

---

## Passenger Guards

Several guards are already fighting elsewhere on the deck.

They do not need individual tokens unless a player directly interacts with them.

Their battle can remain cinematic.

---

## Scavenger Raiders

Start with:

**3 Raiders**

already aboard the passenger vessel.

Suggested positions:

- One near the gangplank.
- One near cargo.
- One moving towards the centre of the deck.

---

## Scavenger Scrapper

Start with:

**1 Scrapper**

aboard the passenger vessel.

Place them near the gangplank.

They are helping secure the boarding position.

---

## Crossing Scavengers

Place:

**2 Raiders**

near the gangplank or preparing to cross.

They have not yet fully joined the fight.

---

## Scavenger Vessel

Begin with:

- Scavenger Boss.
- 1 Raider.
- 1 Scrapper.

Additional scavengers may be visible operating the vessel.

They are not automatically combatants.

---
