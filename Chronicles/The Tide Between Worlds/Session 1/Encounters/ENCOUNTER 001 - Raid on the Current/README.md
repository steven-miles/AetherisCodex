# Encounter 001 - Raid on the Current

> "They were looking for easy prey.
>
> They chose poorly."

---

# Overview

**Raid on the Current** is the first combat encounter of *The Tide Between Worlds*.

It occurs shortly after the player characters awaken in Aetheris, aboard a passenger vessel travelling towards Port Meridian.

A group of desperate scavengers have heard rumours that people arriving from other worlds sometimes carry strange and valuable possessions.

They decide to raid the vessel.

They expect frightened passengers.

Instead, they encounter a group of unexpectedly capable new arrivals.

---

# Encounter Role

This encounter exists primarily to:

- Give the players an immediate opportunity to test their characters.
- Introduce combat within Aetheris.
- Reveal the Void for the first time.
- Demonstrate the importance of player choice during combat.
- Establish that enemies do not always fight to the death.
- Allow the party to begin forming a reputation before reaching Port Meridian.
- Give Port Meridian's authorities reason to recognise the party as unusually capable new arrivals.

The encounter should feel exciting and dangerous without presenting a serious threat of party defeat.

The narrative purpose remains the same regardless of which party is playing it.

Mechanical balance is handled through party-specific encounter variants.

---

# Narrative Position

The encounter occurs during:

**Session 1 - Arrival**

The opening sequence is:

1. The party awakens aboard the passenger vessel.
2. They begin attempting to understand what has happened.
3. The vessel is attacked.
4. The party chooses whether to help.
5. The party sees the Void for the first time.
6. The raid occurs.
7. The scavengers retreat or the encounter resolves another way.
8. The passenger vessel continues towards Port Meridian.
9. Captain Vex Sterling reports the attack upon arrival.
10. Thomas meets the party for the first time on the docks.
11. The party enters the normal Port Meridian arrival process.
12. Their actions during the raid contribute to the Governor taking notice of them.

The raid does not create the Governor's problem.

It simply gives him reason to believe these particular new arrivals may be capable of helping.

---

# Key NPC

## Captain Vex Sterling

Captain Vex Sterling commands the passenger vessel.

Vex is a competent working captain whose primary concern during the raid is keeping the vessel safely within the Current and protecting the people aboard.

Vex is not intended to become a major recurring character.

After reaching Port Meridian, Vex reports what happened to the dock authorities.

That report influences the party's first impression upon the city.

---

# The Scavengers

The attackers are desperate scavengers.

They are:

- Not professional pirates.
- Not connected to the central mystery.
- Not part of a larger conspiracy.
- Not attempting to murder the passengers.

They have simply heard that newly arrived Voidmarked may possess valuable objects from other worlds.

Attacking a vessel on one of the major Currents near Port Meridian is reckless.

Professional pirates would know better.

The scavengers are desperate enough to take the risk.

---

# Encounter Structure

The encounter follows four broad stages:

**Confidence**

The scavengers believe the passengers will surrender their valuables.

↓

**Surprise**

The party proves far more capable than the scavengers expected.

↓

**Survival**

The scavengers realise the raid has failed.

↓

**Retreat**

Loot stops mattering.

They want to get home alive.

---

# Core DM Principles

## Do Not Force Combat

The party is asked for help.

They are never ordered to fight.

---

## Do Not Fight To The Death

The scavengers retreat when they realise the raid has failed.

---

## Do Not Protect The Intended Story

Players may:

- Capture scavengers.
- Destroy the gangplank.
- Board the other vessel.
- Capture the vessel.
- Negotiate.
- Refuse to participate.
- Lose.
- Do something completely unexpected.

Let them.

The Chronicle continues from the result.

---

## Let The Players Be Powerful

The raid exists partly to allow players to discover what their characters can do.

The encounter should be balanced for the party running it, but it does not need to last a fixed number of rounds.

If the party ends the raid quickly:

That is not a failed encounter.

The scavengers simply made a terrible mistake.

---

## Remember What Happened

After the session:

Update the relevant party's Chronicle state.

Record:

- What the players did.
- Who survived.
- What Captain Vex reported.
- Thomas's impression.
- What reached the Governor.
- Any unexpected changes to the world.

If an outcome is significant enough to affect both parties, record it as a shared-world event.

---

# Final Principle

This encounter is not here to determine whether the party is strong enough to begin the Chronicle.

It exists to give the players their first opportunity to discover what their characters can do...

while the people of Aetheris discover it with them.
