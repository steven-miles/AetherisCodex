# Beat 1 — Awakening

## Purpose

Introduce confusion, safety and curiosity.

The players should understand that something impossible has happened, but the world around them treats it as routine.

---

## Emotion

Disorientation

↓

Safety

↓

Curiosity

---

## Camera

Darkness.

Sound before sight.

The audience hears the world before seeing it.

---

## Audio

- Timber creaking.
- Ropes under tension.
- Harbour bells.
- Muffled voices.
- A steady magical engine humming beneath the floor.

---

## Visual

- Dim passenger hold.
- Around twenty people.
- People of every age.
- Some frightened.
- Some calm.
- Morning light enters when the hatch opens.

---

## Resident Focus

Thomas

Patient.

Efficient.

Kind.

Purpose:
To reassure players that they are safe.

---

## Discoveries

- They are not alone.
- This has happened before.
- The world treats this as routine.

---

## Questions Created

- Where am I?
- What happened?
- Why is everyone so calm?
- What is the strange humming?

---

## Handoff

Thomas:

> "Morning."

> "Watch your footing coming up."


# Beat 2 — First Steps

## Purpose

Reveal Aetheris.

Allow players to discover the world rather than having it explained.

---

## Emotion

Curiosity

↓

Wonder

↓

Overwhelm

↓

Belonging

---

## Camera

The players choose where to look.

The world naturally draws their attention.

---

## Audio

- Harbour bells.
- Gulls.
- Laughter.
- Hammers.
- Engines.
- Traders calling.
- Ropes.
- Wind.

---

## Visual

- Bright morning sky.
- The Void beneath the ship.
- A departing skyship.
- Colourful national flags.
- Bright orange docking clamps.
- Crowded harbour.
- A city disappearing into the distance.

---

## Resident Focus

Thomas

Purpose:
Guide without leading.

Wait while the players take everything in.

Never rush them.

---

## World Moments

A departing ship.

Children wave.

Parents wave too.

Everyone pauses briefly.

Ships leaving are respected.

---

## Ordinary Life

A porter moving cargo.

Merchants arguing.

Edith processing paperwork.

Markets opening.

Trade continuing.

The world does not pause for the players.

---

## Questions Created

- How do ships fly?
- What is the Void?
- Where do those ships go?
- What lies beyond the horizon?

---

## Handoff

Thomas quietly counts everyone.

> "Governor's expecting you."

He leads them towards registration.
