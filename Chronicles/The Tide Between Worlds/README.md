# The Tide Between Worlds

## Premise

For reasons unknown, people from countless worlds have begun appearing throughout Aetheris.

Each bears an ancient symbol known as the Voidmark.

No one understands why this is happening.

As entire islands begin disappearing into the Voidsea, the newly arrived strangers find themselves drawn into a mystery that threatens not only Aetheris, but the boundaries between worlds themselves.

---

## Themes

- Wonder before answers.
- Exploration over conquest.
- Curiosity over certainty.
- Hope in the face of the unknown.
- Ordinary people living through extraordinary events.

---

## Emotional Journey

The Tide Between Worlds is designed to make players fall in love with Aetheris before asking them to save it.

The story begins with confusion and uncertainty before gradually expanding into a grand adventure across the Voidsea.

Players should always feel drawn towards the next horizon.

---

## Design Philosophy

This Chronicle should introduce the world through observation rather than exposition.

Players should discover Aetheris by experiencing it.

If something can be shown rather than explained, it should be.

The world should never revolve around the players.

Instead, the players gradually become part of a world that already existed before they arrived.
