# The Aetheris Codex

> A world where the sky is the sea, and the sea is an endless Void.

The Aetheris Codex is the canonical source of truth for the campaign setting **Aetheris**.

## North Star

> Players should always be more excited by what lies beyond the horizon than by what they have already discovered.

Aetheris is built around wonder, exploration, meaningful returns home, and mysteries that are not always meant to be solved.

## Repository Structure

- `World/` — broad setting and cosmological material.
- `Systems/` — setting systems such as travel, reputation, and the Void.
- `Locations/` — islands, settlements, districts, and sites.
- `Residents/` — notable people and recurring figures.
- `Factions/` — political, criminal, religious, and cultural organisations.
- `Guilds/` — major professional institutions.
- `Timeline/` — known history and chronology.
- `PROJECT.md` — development workflow and product boundaries.
- `DESIGN_PHILOSOPHY.md` — practical creative direction.
- `BACKLOG.md` — prioritised work.
- `TECHNICAL_DEBT.md` — temporary assumptions and unresolved implementation work.
- `CANON_DECISIONS.md` — explicit decisions that constrain future writing.
- `LIVING_HISTORY.md` — changes created through future chronicles.

## Product Boundaries

The Codex describes the world independently of any adventuring party.

Campaign planning and session preparation will be maintained separately so that one story can use Aetheris without becoming the definition of Aetheris.

## Current Milestone

**Foundations and First Vertical Slice**

The immediate focus is establishing the minimum world foundations and bringing Port Meridian to a usable level of depth. The wider setting should remain suggestive rather than exhaustively detailed.
