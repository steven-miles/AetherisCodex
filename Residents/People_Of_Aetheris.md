# People of Aetheris

## Purpose

This document introduces the first notable residents of Aetheris.

Residents are more than non-player characters.

They are people who existed before the players arrived and who will continue living their lives long after the players leave.

Every resident should feel as though they belong to the world rather than the campaign.

Each resident should answer four questions:

- **What everyone knows** – Objective truths that any local would recognise.
- **What people believe** – Rumours, assumptions and common opinions.
- **What nobody knows** – The mystery that keeps the resident interesting.
- **What they represent** – The idea or theme they embody within Aetheris.

---

# The Governor

## What everyone knows

The Governor of Port Meridian was appointed by the Guild Council to oversee the world's greatest trading port.

He is an exceptional administrator with an unmatched understanding of trade, taxation and logistics.

Under his leadership, Port Meridian has remained neutral while rival nations continue to trade peacefully within its harbour.

Despite these achievements, he is widely regarded as an uninspiring speaker and an awkward public figure.

---

## What people believe

Many citizens believe the Governor is incompetent.

His inability to communicate confidently has overshadowed many of the practical improvements he has quietly made throughout the city.

Dockworkers complain about him.

Merchants grumble about him.

Politicians criticise him.

Yet the city continues to function.

---

## What nobody knows

The Governor's greatest fear is failure.

Every difficult decision weighs heavily upon him.

Late into the night he often wonders whether everyone else is right—that perhaps he truly isn't good enough for the responsibility he carries.

He continues anyway.

---

## What he represents

**Responsibility.**

The Governor is not a hero.

He is an ordinary man placed in an extraordinary situation, trying to protect civilisation with the only tools he possesses.

---

# The Fortune Teller

## What everyone knows

An elderly fortune teller has lived in Port Meridian for longer than anyone can remember.

She welcomes anyone willing to listen and never refuses a reading.

She has become as much a part of the city as the harbour itself.

---

## What people believe

Some insist she possesses genuine prophetic gifts.

Others believe she is simply remarkably perceptive.

Many swear she predicted events that later came true.

Just as many insist she has been completely wrong before.

No two stories agree.

---

## What nobody knows

Whether she truly sees the future.

She certainly believes in fate.

She already knows of the Voidmarked and seems unsurprised whenever another arrives.

Beyond that...

Nobody can say.

---

## What she represents

**Possibility.**

Every conversation with her should leave people asking questions rather than providing answers.

---

# Barkeep

## What everyone knows

He owns a tavern.

He serves excellent drinks.

He always seems to know when someone needs another.

---

## What people believe

Some claim he has never aged.

Others insist they have seen him in cities impossibly far apart within the same week.

Stories tell of him stepping through mirrors, disappearing behind one bar and emerging behind another somewhere else in the world.

Nobody can ever prove any of it.

---

## What nobody knows

Who he is.

Where he came from.

How long he has existed.

Or even whether "Barkeep" is truly his name.

Whenever someone asks, he simply smiles.

> "Barkeep."

Then changes the subject.

---

## What he represents

**Mystery.**

Not every question in Aetheris is meant to be answered.

---

# The Dockmaster

## What everyone knows

The Dockmaster oversees every arrival and departure in Port Meridian.

Nothing enters or leaves the harbour without passing through their hands.

They know every berth, every schedule and almost every ship that regularly visits Meridian.

The harbour runs because they do.

---

## What people believe

Some say the Dockmaster has never made a mistake.

Others insist they can recognise a ship by its silhouette alone.

Many captains claim they know the harbour better than anyone alive.

Nobody argues.

---

## What nobody knows

The Dockmaster has never wished to become a captain.

Adventure never appealed to them.

They found happiness in certainty.

While others chase distant horizons, they find satisfaction in ensuring every voyage begins and ends safely.

When asked why they never sailed, they simply reply:

> "I see the whole world every day."

---

## What they represent

**Reliability.**

Civilisation only functions because ordinary people quietly do extraordinary jobs.

---

## First Impression

The harbour is alive with noise.

Engines hum.

Dockworkers shout.

Merchants argue over cargo.

Then one calm voice cuts cleanly through the chaos.

> "Berth Twelve."

The shouting stops.

People move.

The harbour continues.

Without another word.
