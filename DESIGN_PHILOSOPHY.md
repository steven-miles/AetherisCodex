# Design Philosophy

These principles translate the Constitution into practical creative direction.

## North Star

> Players should always be more excited by what lies beyond the horizon than by what they have already discovered.

Aetheris succeeds when people look into the distance and ask, *“What is out there?”*

## The Horizon Principle

The known world should imply a much larger unknown world. A few fully realised places are more valuable than a vast catalogue of unfinished names, but the horizon should always suggest countless further voyages.

## Mystery Is Intentional

Some questions have no canonical answer, including for the creator.

Competing stories may all be plausible. Witnesses may be mistaken, dishonest, changed by what they experienced, or telling the truth in ways no one understands.

Unknown does not mean unfinished.

## Reveal Carefully

Do not answer a mystery merely because an answer can be invented. Reveal it only when the revelation creates greater wonder, stronger consequences, or a better mystery.

## The Ship Is Home

A player ship is not merely transport. It should become a recurring location, a record of past adventures, and a home shared with a growing crew.

Repairs, upgrades, scars, keepsakes, routines, and relationships should make the vessel increasingly personal over time.

## Home Makes Adventure Matter

Exploration is more meaningful when there is somewhere worth returning to. Port Meridian and the ship should offer familiarity, relationships, safety, change, and reminders of previous choices.

## Wonder Before Danger

The unknown should first invite curiosity. Horror and danger are stronger when players initially wanted to approach the strange thing they later learned to fear.

## The World Has Rules, Not Explanations

Phenomena may be observable, navigable, and practically understood without their origin ever being known. Sailors can master a current without understanding why empty space behaves like an ocean.

## Depth Before Breadth

Develop the foundations and the first playable vertical slice before expanding the entire setting. Build enough truth to make improvisation reliable, not enough detail to remove surprise.
