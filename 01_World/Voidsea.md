
# The Voidsea

## Core Principle

The Voidsea is not empty.

It is unknowable.

The journey between islands is as important as the destination.

---

## Invisible Currents

Skyships travel by riding invisible currents that weave through the Voidsea.

Experienced navigators can sometimes see these currents under particular lighting conditions.

Trade routes exist because reliable currents have been charted over generations.

Captains speak in terms of currents rather than compass directions.

---

## Voyage Moods

### Calm
Civilisation, trade, fishing fleets, friendly captains, floating inns, music, routine.

### Curious
Strange lights, unexplained phenomena, drifting ruins, impossible weather, new discoveries.

### Hostile
Pirates, predators, storms, damaged ships, dangerous currents, survival.

### Ancient
Rare.

Colossal relics and remnants from a civilisation older than recorded history.

Designed to inspire awe more than combat.

### Living
Travelling markets, festivals, theatre ships, migrating wildlife, weddings, merchants, ordinary life.

These moments remind the players what they're protecting.

---

## Wildlife

The Voidsea supports its own ecosystem.

Most creatures are not hostile.

Examples include:
- Sky whales
- Flying fish
- Giant rays
- Void jellyfish
- Birds nesting on drifting rock
- Creatures that follow ships for food

Wildlife should make the world feel alive rather than dangerous.

---

## Design Goal

Every time the players raise anchor they should feel excited about what they might discover before reaching their destination.
