# The Constitution of Aetheris

> *"Aetheris is not a world to be completed.*
>
> *It is a world to be explored."*

---

## Why This Exists

The Aetheris Codex is not simply a collection of lore.

It is the living record of a world.

This Constitution exists to ensure that no matter how many years pass, how many Chronicles are played, or how many hundreds of articles are written, Aetheris always feels like the same world.

These are not rules for the players.

They are principles for those who build Aetheris.

Whenever a new location, resident, creature, mystery or Chronicle is created, it should be measured against this Constitution.

If it strengthens these principles, it belongs.

If it weakens them, it should be reconsidered.

---

# The Vision

Aetheris is a world of **wonder**.

Its purpose is not to answer every question.

Its purpose is to inspire curiosity.

Every voyage should promise discovery.

Every island should feel like a story waiting to be told.

Every mystery should leave room for imagination.

Every return home should remind adventurers why they chose to leave in the first place.

If players leave the table asking,

> *"What's over the horizon?"*

then Aetheris has succeeded.

---

# The Twelve Principles

## I — Wonder Before Explanation

Wonder is the heart of Aetheris.

Whenever possible, inspire curiosity before providing answers.

Not every mystery exists to be solved.

Some exist simply to remind us that the world is larger than we are.

---

## II — Adventure Begins At The Horizon

The horizon should always be more exciting than the map.

There is always another island.

Another rumour.

Another voyage.

Another impossible story.

Players should never feel that they have seen everything.

---

## III — Mystery Is Precious

Mystery is a finite resource.

Spend it carefully.

Every answer should create another question.

Leave room for imagination.

Trust players to wonder.

---

## IV — The Extraordinary Needs The Ordinary

Most people live ordinary lives.

They farm.

They trade.

They raise families.

They complain about taxes.

Because ordinary life exists, extraordinary moments feel extraordinary.

Magic should inspire wonder, not replace everyday life.

---

## V — Home Matters

Every great journey needs somewhere worth returning to.

Home is where stories are shared.

Where ships are repaired.

Where friendships grow.

Where victories are celebrated.

Where losses are mourned.

Without home...

there is no adventure.

---

## VI — The World Remembers

Nothing is forgotten.

Every meaningful decision should leave an echo.

Sometimes immediately.

Sometimes years later.

Sometimes in another Chronicle entirely.

History belongs to everyone who sails Aetheris.

---

## VII — Humour Gives Weight To Horror

Laughter and fear strengthen one another.

Moments of absurdity make moments of darkness more powerful.

The world should make players laugh.

The world should occasionally unsettle them.

Neither should overwhelm the other.

---

## VIII — No One Is Bigger Than Aetheris

No resident.

No king.

No villain.

No hero.

No Chronicle.

The world existed before them.

It will continue after them.

Every legend becomes another chapter in Aetheris' history.

---

## IX — People Create Legends

Heroes are not born.

They become legendary through their choices.

Aetheris should never assume greatness.

It should reward it.

---

## X — The Journey Is The Reward

Treasure is exciting.

Victory is satisfying.

Discovery is unforgettable.

Players should remember the places they visited and the people they met long after they forget how much gold they earned.

---

## XI — Leave Something Unexplained

Some questions should never receive official answers.

Not because answers do not exist...

but because imagination is more powerful than certainty.

Wonder survives where certainty ends.

---

## XII — Every Chronicle Leaves A Mark

Chronicles should add to Aetheris.

Not replace it.

Every campaign becomes history.

Future generations should discover echoes of those who came before.

The world grows because people lived within it.

---

# Design Rules

These are practical rules used while writing the Codex.

## One Article Answers One Question

Every article should have a single purpose.

Avoid duplication.

Link instead.

---

## Build Depth Before Breadth

Finish today's island before inventing tomorrow's.

Finish today's residents before inventing new ones.

A few memorable places are stronger than a hundred unfinished ones.

---

## Protect Mystery

If explaining something makes it less magical...

don't explain it.

---

## Every Place Has A Story

Locations should exist because people live there.

Not because adventurers need to visit.

The world should feel alive without the players.

---

## Every Resident Wants Something

Nobody exists simply to give quests.

Every resident should have ambitions.

Regrets.

Relationships.

A life beyond the current Chronicle.

---

## Every Choice Should Echo

Consequences should ripple.

Sometimes quickly.

Sometimes years later.

Sometimes in ways nobody expected.

---

## The World Is Always Moving

Ships continue sailing.

Guilds continue trading.

Children grow older.

Governors retire.

Empires rise.

Pirates disappear.

Life continues between adventures.

---

## Finish Before Expanding

A completed harbour is more valuable than ten unfinished kingdoms.

Complete what the current Chronicle needs.

The horizon will still be waiting tomorrow.

---

# The North Star

Whenever uncertainty arises...

ask one question.

> **Does this make players wonder what lies beyond the horizon?**

If the answer is yes...

it probably belongs in Aetheris.

If the answer is no...

ask whether it belongs somewhere else.

---

# The Final Promise

The purpose of The Aetheris Codex is not to create the biggest fantasy world.

It is to create one players never want to leave.

Years after a Chronicle ends...

they should still remember the people they met.

The taverns they visited.

The ships they sailed.

The mysteries they never solved.

And somewhere, just beyond the clouds...

they should still believe another adventure is waiting.

---

# Current North Star

> Players should always be more excited by what lies beyond the horizon than by what they have already discovered.

This statement is the current test for prioritisation. It does not replace the Twelve Principles; it focuses them.

# Deliberate Unknowns

A deliberate unknown is a canon decision that no definitive answer currently exists in the Codex, including for the creator.

It is not a placeholder and it is not unfinished work.

Contradictory testimony, partial evidence, practical expertise, and sincere belief may surround a deliberate unknown without resolving it.
