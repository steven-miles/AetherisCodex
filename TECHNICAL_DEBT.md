# Worldbuilding Technical Debt

This file tracks temporary assumptions, placeholders, contradictions, and material that needs later revision.

Deliberate mysteries belong in canon or the relevant article, not here.

## Open Items

### TD-001 — Skyship Operation

Arcane Currents allow vessels to move as though sailing invisible water, but the practical limits, failure states, and ship components are not yet defined.

**Needed by:** skyship and navigation articles.

### TD-002 — Known Geography

Port Meridian is described as a central hub, but the scale and broad shape of the known world are not yet established.

**Needed by:** trade, travel times, rumours, and regional articles.

### TD-003 — Port Meridian Governance

The city is governed by a guild council and Governor, but selection, terms, voting power, and political tensions remain placeholders.

**Needed by:** Governor's Hall and major guild articles.

### TD-004 — Duplicate Constitution Files

`CONSTITUTION.md` is a concise index while `the_constitution_of_artheris.md` is the full document. Their intended long-term relationship should be clarified or consolidated in a future cleanup.
