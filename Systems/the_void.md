# The Void

## Overview

The Void is the endless nothingness beneath the floating islands of Aetheris.

It is visible from every island edge and every skyship deck: an impossible depth without a known floor, shore, or end.

## Common Knowledge

Falling into the Void is among the oldest fears in Aetheris.

No reliable account explains what happens to those who fall. Most people assume death, but certainty is impossible because bodies do not return.

Sailors secure tools, cargo, and one another with near-religious care. To say that something is “lost to the Void” means it is beyond recovery, proof, and often understanding.

## Disputed Accounts

Across the known skies, people claim to have entered the Void and returned.

Their stories contradict one another. Some describe endless falling. Some insist that something caught them. Others remember places, voices, lights, or nothing at all.

Most are dismissed as liars, drunkards, frauds, or survivors of ordinary accidents who embellished what happened.

The Codex does not establish whether any of them are telling the truth.

## Design Intent

What happens when someone falls into the Void is a deliberate unknown.

There is no current canonical answer, even for the GM. Future stories may introduce testimony, evidence, or apparent survivors without settling the truth.
