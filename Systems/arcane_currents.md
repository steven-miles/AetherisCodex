# Arcane Currents

## Overview

Arcane Currents are invisible magical flows that run through the empty skies of Aetheris. Skyships ride them as seagoing vessels ride waves and tides.

A ship under way rises, settles, rolls, and heels despite there being no visible water beneath it.

## What Can Be Seen

Most of the time, the Currents are invisible.

Under particular light, observers may glimpse faint glints, ripples, or silver distortions moving through otherwise empty space. These appearances are brief and difficult to distinguish from reflection, dust, or imagination.

## Practical Understanding

Experienced sailors learn to feel the Currents through changes in the hull, rigging, wind, pressure, and movement of a vessel. Skilled navigators can identify favourable flows and helmsmen can manipulate a ship's position within them.

Their knowledge is practical rather than complete. A captain may understand how to cross a dangerous Current without knowing why that Current exists.

## Common Knowledge

- Arcane Currents make long-distance skyship travel possible.
- Some routes are safer, faster, or more reliable than others.
- Currents can shift.
- Experienced crews notice changes before untrained passengers do.
- Navigation charts record observed behaviour rather than permanent certainty.

## Unknowns

No accepted explanation accounts for why empty space behaves like an ocean, what creates the Currents, or whether they are a natural, magical, artificial, or living phenomenon.

These questions are intentionally unresolved.

## At the Table

A vessel leaving harbour should feel physically familiar and visually impossible: timbers creak, the deck rocks beneath the passengers, and the ship settles onto waves that cannot normally be seen.
