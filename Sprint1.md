# Sprint 1 - Port Meridian Lives

## Objective

Build everything required to confidently run Session 1.

The objective is not simply to create Port Meridian.

The objective is to make the players desperate to leave it and explore the horizon.

## Deliverables

- Port Meridian guide
- Session 1 NPC roster
- Void Compass lore
- Skyship primer
- Session 1 encounter flow
- Rumours and distant places that tempt exploration

## Emotional Goal

The moment the players leave Port Meridian should feel like leaving the Citadel in Mass Effect.

The horizon should feel limitless.
