# Sprint 1 – Foundations of the Voidsea

## Vision

Sprint 1 is not about writing a campaign.

Sprint 1 is about creating the foundations of Aetheris as a living world that can support many campaigns.

The campaign should emerge naturally from the world rather than the world being built around a single story.

---

## Sprint Goal

Create enough of the world's core systems that any future location, faction or campaign feels like it belongs in Aetheris.

The emphasis is on how the world functions, not on specific adventures.

---

## Design Priorities

### 1. The Voidsea
Define the nature of the Voidsea, its moods, wildlife and its role as the heart of the setting.

### 2. Currents
Establish how invisible currents work, how sailors read them and how they shape trade, exploration and culture.

### 3. Skyships
Define the role of skyships in society, construction, ownership, crews and life aboard.

### 4. Life on the Voidsea
Describe what everyday life is like in a world where every civilisation depends on ships.

Topics include:
- Trade
- Travel
- Navigation
- Sailor culture
- Superstitions
- Common professions
- Communication
- Daily life

### 5. Trade and Exploration
Explain why islands depend upon one another and how exploration drives discovery.

### 6. Wildlife
Create a believable ecosystem that makes the Voidsea feel alive rather than simply dangerous.

### 7. Ports
Only once the previous foundations exist should major ports such as Port Meridian be developed.

---

## Campaign Constraint

Whenever campaign decisions conflict with worldbuilding, the world comes first.

Campaigns should exist because of the world, not the other way around.

---

## Success Criteria

By the end of Sprint 1 we should understand:

- How people live.
- How people travel.
- How people trade.
- What sailors believe.
- Why exploration exists.
- Why the Voidsea feels alive.
- Why players will always want to raise anchor.

Only then do we begin detailed work on Port Meridian and Session 1.
