# Trade

## Purpose

This document explains how trade shapes civilisation throughout Aetheris.

It focuses on why islands choose to trade, how commerce connects the world, and why Port Meridian became the beating heart of the known world.

---

# The Gift of the Islands

Trade is how civilisation shares the gifts of its islands.

No island possesses everything.

Forest islands provide timber.

Mountain islands produce metals.

Crystal islands harvest rare arcane crystals.

Fishing communities bring food from the skies.

Scholar islands create knowledge.

Every island develops around the strengths given to it by its geography, history and people.

Trade allows those strengths to benefit everyone.

Without it, islands survive.

With it, they flourish.
