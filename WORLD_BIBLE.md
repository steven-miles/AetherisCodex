# The Aetheris Codex

> *"A world should still feel alive after the players leave it."*

"Aetheris is not a place for stories to happen. It is a place that feels alive enough that stories naturally emerge from it."

---

## What is Aetheris?

Aetheris is a world first.

The Chronicles are stories told within it.

This repository does not exist to document a single campaign.

It exists to build a living setting capable of supporting many adventures across many years.

---

## The Goal

Every addition to Aetheris should strengthen the feeling that the world already existed before the players arrived and will continue long after they leave.

The players are not the centre of the world.

They become part of it.

---

## Core Pillars

### Wonder

Players should constantly ask:

> "What's over the horizon?"

Curiosity should drive exploration more often than obligation.

---

### Mystery

Not every question should be answered.

Some mysteries exist simply to make the world feel older than the people living in it.

---

### Discovery

Players should discover the world through observation rather than exposition.

Whenever possible:

Show first.

Explain later.

---

### Ordinary People

Civilisation is built by ordinary people doing extraordinary jobs.

Residents should feel like complete people with lives beyond the players' story.

---

### Hope

Although danger exists throughout Aetheris, it is fundamentally an optimistic world.

Journeys represent possibility.

The horizon represents opportunity.

Even in dark times, people continue building, trading, exploring and dreaming.

---

## Repository Philosophy

One document should answer one question.

Large topics should become multiple linked documents.

README files are navigation.

Design notes exist to preserve creative intent.

Canon and design should remain distinct.

---

## Chronicle Philosophy

Chronicles document stories.

The world remains reusable.

Every Chronicle should deepen the setting rather than redefine it.

---

## Final Thought

If players leave a session asking:

> "Where are we going next?"

Then Aetheris has succeeded.

---

# How We Build

Every addition to Aetheris follows the same process.

## 1. Discover

Ask questions.

Challenge assumptions.

Push back.

Find the strongest idea.

## 2. Canonise

Write only what has earned its place.

## 3. Connect

Link documents together.

Everything should feel part of a single living world.

## 4. Review

Ask one final question.

"Does this strengthen Aetheris?"



When designing a Location

• Why would someone choose to live here?
• What makes this place unique?
• What would a child dream about here?
• What would an outsider notice first?

-------------------------

When designing a Resident

• What do they represent?
• What does everyone know?
• What do people believe?
• What does nobody know?
• What is their greatest fear?

-------------------------

When designing a Chronicle

• What should the players feel?
• What should they discover?
• What questions should they leave asking?

## Direct the Player's Eye

Avoid telling players what they notice whenever possible.

Instead, describe events that naturally attract attention.

Movement.

Sound.

People reacting.

The players should feel as though they discovered the world themselves.

## Naming

Names should reflect the role a Resident plays within the world.

Ordinary people should have ordinary names.

Titles should be earned.

Legendary names should be exceptionally rare.

A name should never make a character feel more important than their actions do.

## Ground the Extraordinary

Every extraordinary moment should be surrounded by ordinary life.

Magic feels more magical when contrasted with routine.

Mystery feels older when civilisation continues around it.

The world should never pause simply because something incredible exists within it.
