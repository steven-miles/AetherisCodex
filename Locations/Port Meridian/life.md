# Life in Port Meridian

## Purpose

This document describes the everyday life, customs and culture of Port Meridian.

Rather than explaining the city's history or politics, it focuses on what it feels like to live here and how ordinary people experience the world's greatest harbour.

---

# A City of Journeys

Port Meridian is never still.

Ships arrive from every corner of Aetheris carrying goods, stories, discoveries and rumours. Others leave just as quickly, bound for distant horizons and islands most people will never see.

For many residents, watching a departure is as common as watching a sunrise.

Children stop to watch ships leave.

Merchants pause to see which colours fly from their sails.

Old sailors quietly wonder where the next voyage might lead.

The city lives in anticipation.

Every departure is the beginning of another story.

---

# Respect

The people of Port Meridian respect mastery above status.

A skilled navigator earns admiration.

A master shipwright earns admiration.

An experienced dockworker earns admiration.

People are judged less by what they own than by how well they perform their craft.

Ships may make legends, but competence keeps the city alive.

---

# Childhood

Most children dream of sailing one day.

Many spend afternoons building toy ships, pretending to command great fleets or chasing one another through the streets as admirals and pirates.

To a child born in Meridian, the horizon is a promise.

Whether they ever leave or not, almost every child imagines what lies beyond it.

---

# Departure

Leaving Port Meridian is treated with quiet respect.

Interrupting a ship's departure ritual is considered deeply disrespectful.

Before every voyage, crews salute their engine and ring the ship's bell for good fortune.

Many locals stop what they are doing for a moment as ships leave harbour, watching them disappear into the distance before returning to their own work.

---

# Superstitions

Life beside the Voidsea has created countless traditions.

Some are practical.

Others are impossible to explain.

Common beliefs include:

- Never interrupt a departure ritual.
- Ring the ship's bell before setting sail.
- Always carry rum aboard a voyage.
- Never speak too confidently about the Voidsea.
- A quiet Void is often considered more unsettling than a storm.

Whether these beliefs are true matters less than the comfort they bring to those who sail.

---

# Trade Seasons

The arrival of the great trade fleets is celebrated throughout the city.

Markets become busier.

Taverns stay open later.

New foods, fabrics and curiosities appear from distant islands.

For many families, these arrivals mark the changing of the seasons more clearly than any calendar.

The city comes alive when the fleets return.

---

# Home of the World

The people of Port Meridian are accustomed to strangers.

Every day brings unfamiliar faces, distant accents and tales from places most will never visit.

Few people ask why someone has come to Meridian.

Instead, they ask where they are heading next.

To the people of Port Meridian, every traveller carries another story waiting to be told.

---

# Design Notes

Port Meridian should always feel optimistic.

Although danger exists beyond its docks, the city itself should inspire curiosity rather than fear.

It is a place where ordinary people look towards the horizon with hope, where journeys are celebrated, and where civilisation comes together through trade rather than conquest.

Players should leave Port Meridian feeling excited to discover what lies beyond the next current.
