# Design Philosophy

## Wonder before Answers
The world should always ask more questions than it answers.

If players discover one mystery, they should uncover three more.

Knowledge is earned through exploration, never handed out.

---

## The Horizon Rule
The most exciting place in Aetheris is always the place the players haven't reached yet.

Every port, island, and character should hint at something beyond the horizon.

Players should constantly be making plans about where to sail next.

---

## The Ship is Home
The skyship is not transportation.

It is the players' home.

Every upgrade, crew member, trophy, scar, and story should make the ship feel increasingly personal.

Players should eventually care more about losing the ship than losing gold.

Inspired emotionally by the Normandy and similar "home" ships in fiction.

---

## Every Island is a Story
No island exists simply to contain encounters.

Every island should answer the question:

> Why does this place exist nowhere else?

Players should recognise islands by their identity before their name.

---

## Discovery over Destiny
The players are not important because of prophecy.

They become important because they choose to explore what others cannot.

---

## The Voyage is Never a Loading Screen
Travelling is part of the adventure.

Every voyage should have the potential to deepen the world, the crew, or the players' understanding of Aetheris.

Sometimes that means danger.

Sometimes discovery.

Sometimes simply watching wildlife beneath the hull.

If players ever think "let's skip the sailing", we've broken one of the core promises of the setting.
